Troubleshooting

  • ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED: The configured application listener is stopped, or the approved interface/firewall path is blocking access.
  • Site can't be reached: The phone cannot reach the laptop's IP. Verify both devices are on the same Wi-Fi/hotspot.
  • Hostel Wi-Fi isolation: Some hostel networks block device-to-device communication. Use Laptop Hotspot Mode instead.
  • QR shows localhost or an unexpected address: Stop the service and restart it with Start-Hotspot.cmd so the advertised origin matches the hotspot interface.
